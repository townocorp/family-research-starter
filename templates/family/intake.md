## Existing material

Inventory certificates, scans, photos, GEDCOM exports, stories, interviews,
recordings and eulogies. Record supplier/creator, custody, date, context, rights
and consent for each. Retain originals and independent backups.

## Remembered claims

Record who remembers each person, date or relationship, when they recalled it,
their exact words and any uncertainty. Do not turn recollection into proof.
